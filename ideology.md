# The ideology of That Natural Product Chemist

That Natural Product Chemist is an independent educational publication about how natural product chemistry is actually practiced, interpreted, questioned, and communicated. Its subject is wider than structure assignment and wider than any single analytical technique.

## Scientific scope

The publication may cover source organisms and ecological context, extraction and fractionation, chromatography and isolation, dereplication, NMR and mass spectrometry, optical and complementary spectroscopy, computational tools, structure elucidation, biological evaluation, literature interpretation, laboratory practice, and the professional experience of doing this work.

No technique is presented as sufficient by default. The useful question is what a method contributes, what assumptions accompany it, and how its evidence relates to the rest of the investigation.

## Four forms of publication

### Articles

Articles examine noteworthy scientific papers. They should identify the paper clearly, explain why it merits attention, distinguish what the authors reported from the contributor's interpretation, and discuss the strength, limits, and implications of the evidence.

### Opinions

Opinions are the open editorial space for reasoned positions and practical assessments of tools. Preference and professional judgment are welcome when identified as such and supported by enough evidence and context for a reader to assess or challenge them.

### Exercises

Exercises are evidence-first. Readers meet observations in stages and make a proposal before the final structure is shown. Titles, figures, downloadable data, metadata, and file names must not reveal the answer prematurely. A solution is published only after scientific review; an unfinished solution is described honestly as withheld or in review.

### Examples

Examples are structure-forward. The selected structure remains visible while each step demonstrates what a technique shows, why it was selected, and how it contributes to the interpretation. An Example teaches through guided comparison rather than suspense.

## Evidence, inference, and opinion

Communication should distinguish three layers:

1. **Observation:** what the experiment, source, or publication directly reports.
2. **Inference:** what interpretation is supported by those observations and with what confidence.
3. **Opinion:** a reasoned judgment, preference, or position belonging to the author.

These layers may coexist, but they should not be allowed to blur into one another. Absence of a signal is not automatically proof. Automated output is not automatically a final result. Database identity is not a substitute for experimental support.

## Educational design

Every piece should offer an entry point for a junior researcher without flattening the science for an advanced reader. Plain orientation, explicit learning goals, and staged reasoning come first; acquisition details, complete tables, limitations, references, and downloadable evidence preserve depth.

The reader should be invited to pause, compare, annotate, and disagree. The site should help readers practice scientific judgment rather than merely consume final answers.

## Communication and visual spirit

The visual language should feel like a careful scientific notebook edited for publication: restrained, legible, information-rich, and attentive to hierarchy. Figures are evidence, not decoration. Captions should state the technique, context, and intended observation. Tables should remain readable on small screens. Color should encode meaning sparingly and never be the only carrier of meaning.

Interactive tools should deepen inspection, not gate access. Every interactive dataset needs useful static figures, captions, acquisition context, and a direct data link so that the scientific argument survives without JavaScript or a particular viewer.

## Uncertainty, correction, and review

Uncertainty belongs in the published record. Competing interpretations, overlap, sensitivity limits, incomplete experiments, and unresolved assignments should be named. Corrections should remain visible through revision dates and correction notes rather than silently rewriting history.

Draft analyses remain outside production unless their provisional nature is the explicit subject of publication. Exercise solutions require verification of chemical shifts, multiplicities, integrals, correlations, atom labels, and interpretation before release.

## Openness and responsibility

When rights permit, underlying data should accompany interpretation in sanitized, documented form. Original instrument folders are immutable sources; publication copies must remove private paths, usernames, audit histories, stale sample metadata, and accidental answer leakage.

Contributions are judged by clarity, rights, provenance, evidence, and willingness to revise rather than institutional status. Attribution, licensing, and the limits of educational material should be visible.

## The governing principle

Show enough of the work that another natural product chemist can follow the reasoning, test the claim, identify the uncertainty, and learn something useful even when they disagree with the conclusion.
