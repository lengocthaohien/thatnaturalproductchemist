A quaternary imidazole is not simply an imidazole with a positive charge. Here, the term means a **1,3-disubstituted imidazolium ring**: both ring nitrogens carry carbon substituents, so no N–H proton can be lost to regenerate a neutral imidazole. The positive charge is built into an aromatic natural product. Once regarded as a maca-specific oddity, this moiety now appears in both plant and bacterial natural products.

## A different kind of imidazole

Ordinary imidazole exchanges between neutral and protonated states. In a 1,3-disubstituted imidazolium, that acid–base equilibrium is removed. Six π electrons retain an aromatic ring, while the charge is delocalised particularly across the N1–C2–N3 unit.

This definition also prevents two useful misclassifications. The abundant 2-aminoimidazole alkaloids of marine sponges do not automatically belong here. Nor do hercynine, ergothioneine, and selenoneine: their permanent charge resides in a trimethylammonium side chain, not in the imidazole ring.

The imidazolium C2 position adds another dimension. Base can remove its proton to generate an N-heterocyclic carbene. Mlostoń and co-workers demonstrated this chemistry with lepidilines A and C, trapping the carbenes with sulfur. That is laboratory reactivity, not evidence for carbene formation in vivo.

## One moiety, independent families

Cui and co-workers established lepidilines A and B from maca roots in 2003; C–G followed. Their N-benzyl substitution initially made the class seem taxonomically exceptional.

That exception has disappeared. Yan and co-workers isolated bacillimidazoles A–F from a sponge-associated *Bacillus* in 2022. Discolins followed from *Tenacibaculum discolor*, and bacillimidazole G from probiotic *Bacillus licheniformis* in 2023. Shewazoles from *Shewanella aquimarina* broaden the proposed distribution further, although those MS/MS-based assignments remain tentative. A structural motif is no longer a reliable maca-specific signature.

## Enzymes supply the pieces; chemistry closes the ring

The bacterial families are especially instructive because their imidazolium rings can form without a dedicated ring-forming enzyme. Isotope feeding linked the dimethylated part of the bacillimidazole ring to glucose-derived 2,3-butanedione. In the discolin pathway, DisA decarboxylates phenylalanine to phenethylamine; subsequent condensation forms the imidazolium nonenzymatically. Biomimetic reactions and further work on bacillimidazole G support that chemical logic.

Piedl and co-workers extended this argument beyond culture vessels in 2026. They isolated bacillimidazoles A and E from *Cellulophaga omnivescoria*, reproduced their spontaneous formation, and detected bacillimidazole A on 62% of moon-snail egg collars examined. This is evidence for ecological occurrence, not merely an extraction artefact. It does not establish the same pathway for lepidilines.

The special feature is therefore the moiety, not a single lineage. A permanent cation can recur across unrelated organisms when accessible precursors and simple chemistry converge on the same aromatic ring.
